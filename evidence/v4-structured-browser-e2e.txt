Staking commits GEN to support validator participation and aligns validator incentives with honest network behavior. Delegation lets a holder back a validator without operating infrastructure; it does not remove the need to assess that validator. Spreading delegation across genuinely independent validators can reduce concentration of voting influence and dependence on one operator. Independence matters: several validator names under one controller do not necessarily improve decentralization. One concrete risk is validator downtime or misconduct, which can reduce expected participation rewards or expose a delegator to penalties under the network rules. Delegators should check performance, operator independence and the applicable penalty and withdrawal rules. This is an educational explanation, not a promise of profit.
