How QuestProof works

01 / RULES

Organizers describe quest conditions using natural language.

02 / EVIDENCE

Participants supply a URL and the actual text they submitted.

03 / REVIEW

The Intelligent Contract is designed to use GenLayer's AI execution and validator comparison.

04 / REASON

The contract stores PASS, FAIL or REVIEW with an explanation.

Why GenLayer?

NATURAL LANGUAGE

Beyond keyword checks

Assess whether submitted ideas meet the intent of complex rules.

CONSENSUS

Compare AI outcomes

GenLayer's comparative equivalence principle is designed to compare non-deterministic review outputs.

EXPLAINABILITY

Show the rationale

Return a review reason rather than only a binary result.

Project status & limitations

QuestProof is an experimental MVP. This published frontend runs only a deterministic browser-based simulation and is not connected to the contract. The contract implementation is available on GitHub for testing in GenLayer Studio; its successful on-chain execution has not yet been verified.

Input URLs are never fetched and the app cannot confirm who authored a post, whether it was actually published or reward eligibility. Do not use it for production reward distributions or irreversible decisions.
